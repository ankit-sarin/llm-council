You ask, the council debates

A panel of AI judges weighs in — then a head judge writes the verdict.

The button you click

Imagine you type a hard medical question into a box and hit Submit. In most apps, one AI answers and you cross your fingers. Here, something different happens.

Three LLMs answer at the same time. Then they review each other’s work. Then a fourth, smarter AI reads everything and writes the final answer.

👫
If you only remember one thing

One AI is one opinion. A council of AIs is a panel — with built-in disagreement signals so you know when to trust the answer.

💬
You ask one question

Typed into a simple web form — no command line, no setup.

👥
Three AIs answer in parallel

Each one writes its own answer, independently, without seeing the others.

🏆
A head judge writes the verdict

A smarter AI reads every answer plus every critique and synthesizes one final response for you.

Three stages at a glance

The whole pipeline has three acts. Each one feeds the next, like rounds of a competition show.

1
Initial responses (in parallel)

Each council member receives the same question and writes an answer from scratch. They stream their answers back word by word.

2
Peer reviews (also in parallel)

Each model now reads the others’ answers (anonymized) and grades them on accuracy, insight, and completeness.

3
Chairman synthesis

A separate, more capable model (Claude Opus 4.5) reads everything — answers, reviews, agreement scores — and writes the final answer.

Why a council and not just one AI?

One model can be confidently wrong, biased toward its training data, or just hallucinate a plausible-sounding fact. A council trades a few minutes of compute for a sanity check.

🔍

Redundancy

Three independent attempts at the same question. If two agree and one disagrees, you can see exactly where the disagreement is.

🤔

Cross-check

Each model critiques the others. A weak answer doesn’t just slip through — it gets called out by name before it reaches you.

📜

Synthesis

A smarter chairman model reads the whole transcript and writes one coherent answer — not just an average, but a real verdict.

💡
Aha! moment

Engineers call this pattern ensembling with cross-evaluation. Most production AI systems eventually grow into something like this once a single-model answer is not good enough.

The prompt every model actually sees

Before the debate begins, the Council Engine sends each member the exact same prompt. It is short, deliberately. The constraints do most of the work.

CODE 
prompt = f"""You are a member of an expert council deliberating on the following question.
Provide your best, most thoughtful answer. Be thorough but concise.

QUESTION: {question}

YOUR RESPONSE:"""
PLAIN ENGLISH

We are telling the model: pretend you are on an expert panel.

Two simple rules — be thoughtful, do not ramble.

(Blank line for visual breathing room.)

Drop in whatever question the user typed, right here.

(Another blank line.)

End with a cue that signals “your turn” — the model will keep typing from here.
